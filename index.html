Small Group & Intervention Tracker

This tool is designed for group-level intervention entry that automatically builds individual student histories.

Privacy: Data is saved locally in your browser on this device. It is not sent to an online server. Follow your school or district policies for student information.

Suggested workflow

1. Create intervention groups and meeting days.
2. Add students and assign each student to one group. Archive groups when they are no longer active; historical data remains available.
3. Use Daily Sessions to record instruction, attendance, mastery, and notes.
4. Use Progress Monitoring for fast whole-group score entry or individual student entry.
5. Review trends in Student Reports and print or save an individual report as a PDF when needed.

Daily mastery scale

1 — Emerging: Beginning to demonstrate the skill; requires significant support.
2 — Developing: Demonstrates the skill with prompting or support.
3 — Proficient: Demonstrates the skill independently with general consistency.
4 — Mastery: Demonstrates the skill independently and consistently.
